# Review

## Scope and independence

Artifact or revision reviewed, requested write boundary, author and reviewer. State whether this is independent review or author self-review.

## Findings

For each actionable finding: severity, location, trigger, consequence and evidence. Separate a verified failure from a reasoned finding or unverified concern. Omit speculative padding.

## Verification

Checks performed, results and checks that could not run.

## Conclusion and limits

Which claims the evidence supports, remaining risk and the next useful action. Do not modify the artifact under review unless a fix was requested.
